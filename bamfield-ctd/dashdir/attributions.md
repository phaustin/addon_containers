**Data.** CTD casts from the UBC EOSC 473/573 field school at Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre,
each February from 2001 to 2026 (none in 2010 or 2019–2022), taken with Sea-Bird profilers and,
in 2025, an AML-6 (read with [AML-CTD-Tools](https://github.com/HalBradbury/AML-CTD-Tools)).
Temperature and salinity are shown as Conservative Temperature and Absolute Salinity (TEOS-10;
IOC, SCOR and IAPSO, 2010). Oxygen is in µmol L⁻¹. Fluorescence is an uncalibrated relative
index (2012 omitted). Casts with impossible positions, and values outside plausible ranges,
are left out.

**Layout** adapted from the EOSC 372 ocgy-dataviewer.
