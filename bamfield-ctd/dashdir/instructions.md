### The Bamfield CTD archive, 2001–2026

**About the profiles.** At each site the black line is the **mean across years** and the grey
band is **± 1 standard deviation across years**. The spread between profiles is year-to-year
change in the water, not error in the data: bad values are removed before anything is averaged.
Each year's casts at a site are averaged first, so a year counts once however many casts it has.
The top metre is left out of every cast, archive and uploaded, because it often holds the
surface soak; profiles start at 1.5 m.
Some of the spread may come from the instruments, though. The CTD changed over the years
(Sea-Bird SBE19 2001–2016, SBE25 2009–2025, SBE19plus 2014–2026, and an AML-6 in 2025 whose
oxygen sensor is an optode, not an electrode), so treat a difference that lines up with a change
of instrument with care. Hovering over a cast shows which CTD took it.

Every field-school CTD cast in Barkley Sound, grouped into **sites** by position (casts within
0.5 km of a common centre), because the archive's 600-odd station labels are not consistent from
year to year. Each site has a number and the water body it is in, with its typical cast depth;
the number is what tells sites apart. Hovering over a site shows the labels students actually
logged there.

- **One site, all years.** Click a site on the map. Every cast there is drawn, coloured by year, over
  the mean across years (black) and ± 1 standard deviation across years (grey band). Hover over
  another site to see its mean as a dashed line.
- **One year, several sites.** Choose a year; only the sites visited that February are dark. Click
  sites to add or remove them.
- **The grid** (right) shows which sites were visited in which years, for sites with five or more
  years. Click a cell to open that site in that year. Gaps in the grid are the reason the
  year-to-year plot at the bottom has to remove the site effect first.
- **Several variables on one depth axis** (below the profiles): pick casts from the selected
  sites and the variables to overlay. Colour is the variable, line style the cast. The T–S
  diagram below it uses the same casts and depth range.
- The depth slider limits the depth axis. Scroll to zoom the map. The camera icon above the
  profiles saves them as a PNG.
